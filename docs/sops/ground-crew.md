## 2. Welcome

### 1. Overview
1. Thank you for stepping up and fulfilling a leadership position in the Club. As a Staff member, Club members will look to you for leadership and guidance. You should strive to provide that, in order to make our members’ experience here a great one!

2. Everything you do here should focus on providing a quality experience for TPC members. We ask you to lead by example, in words and actions. Your contributions to the Club in this manner are greatly appreciated and valued. You have a direct impact on retaining members and growing the Club.

3. Let’s make this Club an excellent place for flight sim enthusiasts to be a part of.
### 2. Ways to help TPC Succeed
   1. Be in voice channels as often as is practical. If a voice channel is getting pretty full see if you can start another one with one or two people.
      
   2. Help in recruiting efforts.
      
   3. Talk to new and old members in chat and make them feel welcome.
      
   4. Volunteer where possible elsewhere in the club.
      
   5. Be kind
      
   6. Be available

## 3. Standard Procedures

### 1. Overview
   1. Ground Crew is an imperative role within The Pilot Club Discord server as they act as the moderators and handle conflicts and other offences that happen within the discord server and the club as a whole.
      
   2. Ground Crew members should always exercise their best judgment as every situation is different but it is important to remember that abuse of power will never be acceptable. Always try de-escalating first unless the opportunity to do so does not exist

### 2. Documentation of Cases / Infractions
   1. When handling cases / other infractions with The Pilot Club Inc. members, special care should be taken to properly document the instance and what steps were taken to resolve the issue.
      
   2. The following documentation shall be created when working within your capacity as a ground crew member:
      
      1. An audit log note shall be created on the users profile within the Flight Crew Portal explaining the situation and what actions were taken on the member
      
   3. The following documentation can be used to accompany the audit log note but are not required when creating the documentation:
      
      1. A “Member Report” ticket within the discord server under the support channel with the member added to it (further explanation later in this document) with explanation of the infraction and conversation with the member
      
      2. A PDF document, created by exporting a word document from google drive, with the outline of the infraction and what steps were taken to resolve the issue.
      
   4. Other forms of documentation may be approved via the Chief Technology Officer and/or Chief Administrative Officer on a case by case basis.

### 3. Typical Ways to handle a case
   1. Breach of Club Standard Operating Procedures
      
      1. Discuss the violation privately with said individual. This kind of conversation should not be carried out in such a way that it is witnessed by other club members.
      
      2. Do not engage in "witch hunt" type behavior. Staff should not actively seek out trouble from an individual. We may not always like every member of the club but it is unfair to target individuals due to personal feelings.
      
   2. Causing a disturbance or being a nuisance in a voice or text channel.
      1. Privately message them or pull them into another voice channel and make an attempt to calm them down or change their behavior.
      
      2. If they are unwilling to work with you or you believe that once the conversation ends no change will be made you should time them out of the server. Let an Air Marshal know that this has happened so they can step in if problems progress.
      
      3. If they return and continue causing problems, the option of a ban should be discussed with an Air Marshal. Bans are not given out easily and are only given after careful consideration.

   3. Causing Issues or Breaking Rules on the VATSIM Network
      1. Immediately pull them aside and let them know what the issue is. Try to be informative as to why their actions are a problem.
      
      2. If the offender cannot be contacted, try to let the controller know that you are doing everything you can to get into contact with the offending party and they are acting on their own accord outside the scope of the club.
      
      3. If the offender refuses to cease their behavior on frequency they should be timed out from the discord and the controller should be notified. Air Marshals should also be notified so that further measures can be discussed.
   4. Banning Members
      1. Bans will need to be run by Air Marshals unless it is extremely time sensitive and they are causing huge problems. If you need to ban someone, please take as much evidence as you can prior to the ban. When you ban someone it will delete their messages within the last 48 hours so make sure to get things as quickly as possible.
      
      2. Bans are given out in special circumstances in which there is no other option and all other reasonable measures have been taken to stop unwanted behavior.
      
      3. Bans need to be discussed and issued or issuance approval by an Air Marshal.
       
      4. When someone returns from a ban staff should be watchful of further offenses.
      
### 4. Useful Bot Commands

   1. /mute {member}

   2. /unmute {member}

   3. $lockdown (channel) (time)

   4. $unlockdown (channel) **Notify an Air Marshal when this command is run so we can verify correct permissions**

   5. Timeout (right click and follow the prompts)

### 5. TPC Discord Server Ticketing System

   1. TPC uses “Ticket Tool” to allow members to report issues with other members and various other systems.
   
   2. Ground Crew members can use this system to document a conversation between them and a member. The following steps can be taken to use this system effectively:
   
      1. Open a ticket with the subject being “Member Report”. This will open a ticket that Ground Crew can see and take actions on.
      
      2. Use the ticket tool “/add” command to add a member (with the user dropdown) which will need to be given by user id. User Id’s can be found like this: https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/206346498-Where-can-I-find-my-User-Server-Message-ID
      
      3. Once the member has been added, you can continue the conversation as you need.
      
      4. After the conclusion of the conversation, make sure to close and delete the ticket.
      
         1. To get a transcript, you can go the “Support Ticket Transscripts” channel and copy the message link OR use the button to generate a link within the ticket tool system.
